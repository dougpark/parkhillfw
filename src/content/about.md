# Welcome to the New Park Hill Directory
We designed the neighborhood directory to make staying connected easier, safer, and more comfortable for everyone in our community. By focusing on accessibility, modern security, and clean visual design, the new site removes friction so you can quickly find the information you need.

## Designed for Effortless Usability
    
**Touch-Friendly Controls:** Large buttons, generous tap targets, and interactive cards make navigation simple on phones, tablets, and computers alike—no precise or double-clicking required.

**Clear, High-Contrast Text:** Built with ADA-compliant contrast ratios and clean typography to ensure text remains crisp and readable without straining your eyes.

**Clutter-Free Visual Space:** Expanded white space and simplified layouts keep pages clean and organized, letting you focus on the details that matter.

**Adaptive Color Themes:** Switch comfortably between light and dark modes to reduce screen glare and tailor the display to your sight preferences.

**Simple Navigation:** Card-based layouts, intuitive filter chips with visual indicators, and clear breadcrumb trails keep you oriented so you never feel lost.

## Secure & Stress-Free Access

**Passwordless "Magic Link" Sign-In:** Skip the headache of remembering passwords or dealing with account lockouts. Sign in safely using a direct link sent straight to your email.

**Extended Login Sessions:** Stay signed in on your trusted personal devices longer, reducing the need to log in repeatedly.

**Protected Resident Data:** Secure, encrypted document and data storage ensures sensitive directory details and neighborhood files remain strictly private within our community.
